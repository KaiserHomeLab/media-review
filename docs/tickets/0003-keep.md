# 0003: Keep from Media Ops

**Blocked by:** 0001

## What to build

- API: Keep a queued Title.
- Media Ops: a Keep button on the Review Queue card. This is a Media Ops PR.
- Keep takes the Title out of the Review Queue and clears all its Downvotes, so it starts again from zero. Record the decision in the Title's history.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] After Keep, the Title is gone from the queue and has no Downvotes.
- [ ] Voters can Downvote it again, and it can queue again.
- [ ] Keep on a Title that isn't queued is rejected.
- [ ] Keep appears in the Title's history on later Review Queue entries.
